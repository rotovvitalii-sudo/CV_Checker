# Head of Product — Lumen Learn

_Вымышленная вакансия для демо._

Lumen Learn is a subscription learning platform for adults who want to manage their money better. We have 40K learners
and we are now launching **Lumen Academy**: guided learning paths that our partner banks embed into their onboarding.

**What you will do**
- Own the product strategy and roadmap for Lumen Academy, from first pilot to 10 partner banks
- Design learner journeys that move a new bank customer from sign-up to first action
- Work with partnerships and sales on the B2B2C model; set activation and retention metrics
- Lead a team of 6: two PMs, design and content; hire as we grow
- Use AI tools to speed up content production

**What we are looking for**
- 7+ years in product, 2+ leading a team
- You have launched an academy, LMS or education-led onboarding before
- Subscription monetization experience
- Strong analytics: funnels, cohorts, A/B tests
- English C1; Portuguese or Spanish is a plus

**Nice to have**: fintech or banking partnerships, founder experience.
